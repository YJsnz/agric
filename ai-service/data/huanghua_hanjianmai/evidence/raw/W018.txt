微垄覆膜侧沟播宽苗带旱碱麦正负压排种器设计与试验

摘要：
旱碱麦是盐碱耕地的主要植作物之一,在旱碱地资源化利用中占据重要地位,但适配其播种作业的农艺技术与农机装备较为匮乏,导致播种质量不高。针对盐碱地旱碱麦在雨养旱作条件下产量低、缺少适宜种植模式和机具的问题,提出了一种微垄覆膜侧沟播宽苗带种植农艺技术,适用于干旱半干旱地区或降水不稳定区域的作物种植场景,并配套研制了相关的精量播种机械装备。同时,为了更好地适配旱碱麦种子的播种需求,结合气力式排种器作业速度快、通用性好、不易伤种等优点,创新性设计了一种正负压式宽苗带旱碱麦精量排种器。通过理论分析,设计了排种器的关键部件和重要参数。以沧麦6002为研究对象,借助JPS-12试验台,通过单因素试验研究了吸种负压、滚筒转速和落种高度3个因素对排种器性能的影响;通过响应面试验,得到试验因素最佳组合为吸种负压3.35 kPa、滚筒转速18.5 r/min、落种高度121 mm,在此参数组合下排种器的合格率、漏播率、重播率的模型预测值分别为81.20%、9.46%、9.36%;台架验证试验表明,测量值均在上述模型预测值的95%置信区间内,排种合格率、漏播率、重播率满足旱碱麦宽苗带条播农艺要求与田间实际生产作业指标,可适配微垄覆膜侧沟播宽苗带种植模式。

Abstract：
Dry-alkali wheat was one of the main planting crops in saline-alkali farmland, which played an important role in the resource utilization of dry-alkali land. However, the agronomic technology and supporting agricultural machinery and equipment suitable for its sowing operation were relatively scarce, resulting in low sowing quality. Aiming at the problems of low yield and lack of suitable planting patterns and machines for dry-alkali wheat in saline-alkali land under rain-fed and dry-farming conditions, an agronomic technique of micro-ridge filmmulching side furrow sowing and wide seedling belt planting was proposed, which was suitable for crop planting scenarios in arid and semi-arid areas or unstable precipitation areas, and related precision seeding machinery and equipment were developed. At the same time, in order to better adapt to the seeding needs of dry alkali wheat seeds, combined with the advantages of pneumatic metering device, such as high-speed operation, good versatility and not easy to hurt seeds, a positive-negative pressure wide seedling belt dry alkali wheat precision metering device was innovatively designed.Through theoretical analysis, the key components and important parameters of the seed metering device were designed. Taking Cangmai 6002 as the research object, with the help of JPS-12 test bench, the effects of suction negative pressure, drum rotation speed and seed falling height on the performance of seed metering device were studied by single factor test. Through the response surface test, the best combination of test factors was obtained as follows : suction negative pressure 3.35 kPa, drum rotation speed 18.5 r/min, and seed falling height 121 mm. Under this parameter combination, the model prediction values of the qualified rate, missed seeding rate, and replay rate of the seed metering device were 81.20%, 9.46%, and 9.36%, respectively.The bench verification test showed that the measured values were within the 95 % confidence interval of the predicted values of the above model. The seeding qualified rate, missed seeding rate and replay rate met the agronomic requirements of wide-seedling strip sowing of dry-alkali wheat and the actual production operation indicators in the field, and could be adapted to the wide-seedling strip planting mode of micro-ridge mulching and side-furrow sowing.
