摘要：
盐碱地是我国重要的后备耕地资源,其开发利用对保障粮食安全和促进农业可持续发展具有重要意义。旱碱麦作为适宜盐碱旱作区种植的特色粮食作物,具有耐盐碱、抗旱、适应性强等特点,是推进盐碱地农业化利用的核心作物。本文围绕盐碱地改良与旱碱麦生产需求,系统梳理了盐碱地改良技术、旱碱麦种植技术及其配套机械装备的研究现状。重点分析了化学改良、生物改良和物理改良等盐碱地治理技术及其配套装备,以及起垄覆膜、沟播避盐、节水保墒、水肥调控等旱碱麦关键种植技术,并总结了改良剂施用、秸秆还田、深松整地、覆膜播种、沟播施肥、水肥一体化和田间管理装备的发展进展。研究发现:盐碱地改良仍存在机理研究匮乏、协同治理水平偏低和装备适应性较差等问题;旱碱麦生产存在播种质量稳定性不足、农机农艺融合不深和智能化程度较低等难题。未来应加强盐碱地综合治理与旱碱麦适生栽培研究,推进专用化、集成化、智能化装备研发,促进现代信息技术与农机装备深度融合,为盐碱地高效利用和旱碱麦产业发展提供参考。

Abstract：
Saline-alkali land represented an important reserve cultivated land resource in China, and its development and utilization were of great significance for safeguarding food security and promoting sustainable agricultural development. Dryland saline-alkali wheat, as a characteristic grain crop suitable for cultivation in saline-alkali dry farming regions, exhibited strong salt-alkali tolerance, drought resistance, and environmental adaptability, making it a core crop type to promote the agricultural utilization of saline-alkali land. Focusing on the requirements of saline-alkali land amelioration and dryland saline-alkali wheat production, this paper systematically reviewed the research progress in saline-alkali land improvement technologies, dryland saline-alkali wheat cultivation techniques, and associated agricultural machinery and equipment. EmpHasis was placed on saline-alkali land reclamation technologies and supporting equipment, including chemical amelioration, biological amelioration, and physical amelioration, as well as key cultivation techniques for dryland saline-alkali wheat, such as ridge formation and plastic-film mulching, furrow sowing for salt avoidance, water-saving and moisture-conservation cultivation, and water-fertilizer regulation. The development progress of machinery and equipment for soil amendment application, straw returning, subsoiling and land preparation, film-mulched seeding, furrow sowing and fertilization, integrated water-fertilizer management, and field management was also summarized. The review indicated that saline-alkali land amelioration still faces several challenges, including insufficient mechanistic research, li-mited synergistic remediation capacity, and poor equipment adaptability. Dryland saline-alkali wheat production was constrained by insufficient stability of seeding quality, insufficient integration of agronomic practices and agricultural machi-nery, and a relatively low level of intelligentization. Future research should strengthen integrated saline-alkali land ma-nagement and adaptive cultivation of dryland saline-alkali wheat, promote the development of specialized, integrated, and intelligent agricultural machinery and equipment, and facilitate the deep integration of modern information technologies with agricultural machinery, thereby providing a reference for the efficient utilization of saline-alkali land and the indust-rial development of dryland saline-alkali wheat.
